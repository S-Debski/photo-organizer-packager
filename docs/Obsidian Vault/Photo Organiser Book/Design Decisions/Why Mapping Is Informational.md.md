# Why Mapping Is Informational
  
Mapping in Photo Organizer is designed as **context**, not a required workflow step.
  
---

## ✅ What Mapping Is For

- Visualising where photos were taken

- Revealing GPS quality issues early

- Supporting Organize decisions later

---

## ❌ What Mapping Is Not
  
- Not a primary selection tool

- Not required for Organize

- Not guaranteed to show street‑level detail
  
---
  
## 🧠 Key Principle

> Mapping should never imply more precision than the metadata provides.

Honest visualisation builds user trust.
